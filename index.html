Research Biography

I am working as a domain expert in Viswire Simulation and Training Pvt. Ltd. Prior to this I worked as an Institute Post-Doctoral Researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay. I completed my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee within the Human FRST Lab, focusing on the Behavioral Assessment of Distracted Pedestrians at Intersections .

My core research passion lies at the intersection of human factors, active road safety, intelligent transportation systems (ITS), and external Human-Machine Interfaces (eHMIs). I combine cutting-edge empirical methodologies—such as Virtual Reality (VR) simulators, wearable eye trackers, Markov and predictive modeling, survival analysis, and machine learning—to design and deploy real-world assistive technologies.
