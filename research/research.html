Key Research Projects

Development of smart assistive systems for safer road crossing of pedestrians in smart cities

Dec 2024 – Ongoing | iHub DivyaSampark IIT Roorkee

Lead development of hardware/software prototypes for warning pedestrians in real time to prevent vehicle-pedestrian conflict at conflict points.

Gaze Mapping and Attention Analysis for Pedestrian Safety

Jan 2025 – Ongoing | IIT Roorkee

Develop an automated gaze fixation mapping algorithm for mapping the fixations obtained from the user’s gaze and the world video captured by the eye tracker.

Study of the design of different eHMIs on vehicle in different traffic condition

April 2025 – Ongoing | IIT Indore & TU Eindhoven Collaboration

International collaborative initiative investigating and understanding the subjective differences among various types of eHMI for different vehicle types in the Netherlands and India.

Pedestrian safety in mixed traffic conditions: Probing executable solutions to enhance vulnerable road users’ safety using a pedestrian simulator

Dec 2020 – June 2023 | SERB funded Project

As part of Ph.D assessed the impact of various distractions among pedestrians using various fabricated test case scenarios using state-of-the-art pedestrian simulator setup and wearable eye tracker . Designed and developed assistive technologies to alert pedestrians at both unsignalized intersections (Haptic Alert Wristwatch System, HAWS) and signalized intersections (Traffic Signal Actuated Illuminated Crosswalk, TSAIC), to make road crossing safer for pedestrians.
